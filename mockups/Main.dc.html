Yes — if your chatbot deals directly with people, you have to make clear it is an AI, and you have to do it by 2 August 2026. The duty sits on you as the provider, it applies at the moment of first interaction, and the only way out is if it would already be obvious to a reasonable person. Labelling AI-generated content is a separate duty that may also catch you.

Users must be «informed that they are interacting with an AI system» art.50.1

No duty where it is «obvious from the point of view of a natural person who is reasonably well-informed» art.50.1

Synthetic output must be «marked in a machine-readable format» art.50.2

«at the latest at the time of the first interaction» not found in any retrieved provision

art.50.1
binding · in force
Transparency obligations for providers and deployers of certain AI systems

Providers shall ensure that AI systems intended to interact directly with natural persons are designed and developed in such a way that the natural persons concerned are informed that they are interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious from the point of view of a natural person who is reasonably well-informed, observant and circumspect, taking into account the circumstances and the context of use.
